IPA Natural Class Builder v1

- New standalone app.
- Uses only the symbols in the supplied LING 220 Feature Chart.
- 25 consonants + 12 vowels = 37 symbols.
- LABIAL, CORONAL, and DORSAL are unary.
- All other feature-chart features are binary.
- Feature selection live-filters the IPA inventory to a natural class.
- Consonants and vowels are displayed in IPA-style charts.
